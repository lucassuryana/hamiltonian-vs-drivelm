The ego vehicle may choose one combination of:

Speed:

- not moving
- driving slowly

- driving fast
- driving very fast

Direction:

- going straight

- slightly steering to the right

Q16. Considering all relevant objects together, determine the ego vehicle's final Speed and Direction.

The chosen Speed and Direction must:

- be physically reachable from the historical ego behaviour;
- remain as close as possible to the historical behaviour whenever safe;
- minimize the overall collision risk;
- satisfy the strongest planning constraints identified in Q13–Q15.

Speed and Direction are independent fields and must both always be selected from the lists above.

If the chosen Speed is "not moving", Direction should still describe the vehicle's heading.
Never use "not moving" as a Direction.

State the planning priority objects in descending order of influence on the final decision.
