Reachability Constraints

Only one level of change in Speed and one level of change in Direction
is physically reachable from the historical ego behaviour.

Reachable Speed

not moving
→ not moving, driving slowly

driving slowly
→ not moving, driving slowly, driving with normal speed

driving with normal speed
→ driving slowly, driving with normal speed, driving fast

driving fast
→ driving with normal speed, driving fast, driving very fast

driving very fast
→ driving fast, driving very fast

Reachable Direction

steering to the left
→ steering to the left, slightly steering to the left

slightly steering to the left
→ steering to the left, slightly steering to the left,
  going straight

going straight
→ slightly steering to the left, going straight,
  slightly steering to the right

slightly steering to the right
→ going straight, slightly steering to the right,
  steering to the right

steering to the right
→ slightly steering to the right, steering to the right
